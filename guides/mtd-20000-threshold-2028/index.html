MTD £20,000 threshold (from April 2028)

What the third Making Tax Digital for Income Tax wave means in practice.

Not tax advice. Unofficial summary of published GOV.UK thresholds. Verify before you act.

Clear answer

If your qualifying income is over £20,000 for tax year 2026 to 2027, you will need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2028 (unless exempt).

This sits below the earlier waves: over £50,000 → from 6 April 2026; over £30,000 → from 6 April 2027.

Worked example

Landlord + small side hustle. In 2026/27 you expect:

  • Property rents received (gross): £18,000
  • Self-employment turnover: £4,500
  • Combined qualifying income: £22,500 (over £20,000)

On those figures you would be in the April 2028 wave. Always use GOV.UK’s definition of what counts (and what does not, such as partnership profit share as an individual partner).
