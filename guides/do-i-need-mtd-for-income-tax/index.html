Do I need Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?

A calm checklist of who is in scope and which threshold wave applies.

Not tax advice. Unofficial helper. Prefer the official GOV.UK checker when in doubt. Rules and exemptions change.

Clear answer

You need Making Tax Digital for Income Tax if all of the following apply: you are a sole trader or landlord registered for Self Assessment; you get income from self-employment or property (or both); and your qualifying income is over the published threshold for the relevant tax year.

Threshold waves (published)

  • Qualifying income over £50,000 for 2024 to 2025 → use MTD from 6 April 2026
  • Qualifying income over £30,000 for 2025 to 2026 → use MTD from 6 April 2027
  • Qualifying income over £20,000 for 2026 to 2027 → use MTD from 6 April 2028

You still submit a Self Assessment return for the tax year before you start MTD. HMRC may write to you, but it remains your responsibility to check.

Worked example

You are a sole trader on Self Assessment. Your 2024/25 self-employment turnover (before expenses) was £62,000. Property income: £0.

  • Qualifying income £62,000 is over £50,000
  • You should use MTD for Income Tax from 6 April 2026

If instead your 2025/26 turnover will be £35,000 (over £30,000), the start date linked to that year is 6 April 2027.
