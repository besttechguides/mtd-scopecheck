MTD £50,000 threshold (from 6 April 2026)

The first mandatory Making Tax Digital for Income Tax wave for higher qualifying incomes.

Not tax advice. Unofficial summary. Prefer GOV.UK if anything is unclear.

Clear answer

If you are a sole trader or landlord on Self Assessment and your qualifying income for 2024 to 2025 was over £50,000, you should use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2026.

How HMRC frames this wave

HMRC reviews Self Assessment returns and may write to people who appear in scope. GOV.UK is clear that not getting a letter does not remove the obligation — you still need to check and prepare.

Later waves: over £30,000 (2025/26 figures) from 6 April 2027; over £20,000 (2026/27 figures) from 6 April 2028. See the £20,000 guide.

Worked example

Self-employed consultant. 2024/25 turnover £71,000; expenses £28,000; profit £43,000.

  • Qualifying income for MTD looks at turnover £71,000 (before expenses)
  • Over £50,000 → start date 6 April 2026
