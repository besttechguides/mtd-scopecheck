Making Tax Digital for Income Tax — FAQ

Who needs to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?

MTD for Income Tax is aimed at sole traders and landlords registered for Self Assessment whose qualifying income is over the published thresholds for the relevant tax year. Always confirm on GOV.UK.

What are the MTD income thresholds and start dates?

Published waves use qualifying income (turnover / gross before expenses) of over £50,000 from 6 April 2026, over £30,000 from 6 April 2027, and over £20,000 from 6 April 2028. Verify current figures on GOV.UK before you act.

Does partnership profit count toward qualifying income?

Partnership profit share as an individual partner does not count in this checker. See GOV.UK qualifying income guidance.

Is MTD ScopeCheck official HMRC software?

No. This is an unofficial helper with a printable readiness checklist. It is not tax advice and is not affiliated with HMRC. Prefer the official GOV.UK checker when in doubt.

What if the checker says I may be in scope?

Confirm on GOV.UK, keep digital records, and compare recognised compatible software before your start date.
